A toddler in a plain cotton romper takes a first uncertain step toward the camera, arms out; in the near foreground, the blurred shoulder of a kneeling parent. Cold steel-blue window light from the left, a faint burgundy lamp in the background, pale silver highlights on the wooden floor. Shallow depth of field, 35mm anamorphic lens, natural film grain, muted desaturated palette, intimate and photorealistic. Clean unmarked surfaces.
