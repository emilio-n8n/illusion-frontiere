Underexposed 35mm film still from a dark cinematic documentary. A vast cold government control room at night, most of the frame in deep shadow. A lone man in a heavy wool coat, seen from behind, stands close to the camera in the left foreground, his head and shoulders a near-black silhouette rimmed by pale light. He faces a huge projection screen across the room; the screen is the only strong source, pale warm grey, slightly blown out, showing a faint analog world map. Steel-blue walls, brushed metal cabinets, tangled cables, a single small burgundy desk lamp. Thin volumetric haze, dust in the projector beam, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, foreground desk blurred, heavy natural film grain, muted desaturated palette. Clean unmarked surfaces.
